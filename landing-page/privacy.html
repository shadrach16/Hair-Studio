Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Introduction

Hair Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application and website.

Information We Collect

  • Photos: When you use the try-on feature, you upload or capture photos. Your original photo and the generated result are stored with your account so that your past looks remain available to you in the app. They are retained until you or we delete them — see Retention and deletion.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we store your email address and your credit balance. You can try the app as a guest without creating an account; a guest session still stores the photos and results described above.
  • Device information: We may collect basic device data (OS version, device model) for app compatibility and crash reporting.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how people interact with our website. This collects anonymised usage data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the hairstyle try-on service
  • To keep your past looks available to you in the app
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs and crashes

Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal data, and we do not use your photos for advertising or to train any model.

We do share your photos with two service providers who process them on our behalf:

  • Google — the Gemini image model, which generates the new hairstyle from your photo.
  • Cloudinary — image hosting and delivery.

They process your photos only to provide those services to us.

Retention and deletion

Your photos and generated results are kept with your account. They are not deleted automatically once a generation completes.

To have them deleted, email support@hairstudioapp.com and we will remove your photos, your results and your account data.

Data Security

All data transmissions are encrypted using TLS. We apply standard security practices to protect the information we hold.

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of your data
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Contact us with any privacy concern

Children's Privacy

Our app is rated for ages 3+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We update this policy when what we actually do changes. The August 2026 revision corrected an earlier statement that photos were deleted immediately after generation; they are retained with your account, as described above.

Contact Us

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at support@hairstudioapp.com.
