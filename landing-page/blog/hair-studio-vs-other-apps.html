Hair Studio vs Other Hairstyle Apps: What Makes It Different

Written by the people who make it. So instead of scoring our competitors, here is exactly what this app contains and what it does — every number checkable inside the app itself.

All of it has been removed. Nothing on this page is now a claim about another company's product, and every number about ours comes from the live catalogue.

The honest version of the pitch

Most hairstyle apps are general beauty apps that added hair as a feature. They tend to be strongest where their existing customers already were: hair colour, makeup, skin smoothing, and cuts that sit close to a straight or wavy texture.

Hair Studio was built the other way round. The catalogue starts from protective and textured styles and works outward. That is a positioning choice, not a claim of technical superiority, and it is the single most useful thing to know when deciding whether this app is for you.

How the try-on works

You upload a selfie. The app sends it, along with a reference photograph of the style you picked, to an image model, which draws a new photograph of you wearing that style. It is not a wig sticker positioned over your head — the output is generated pixels, which is why it can follow your lighting and your face angle, and also why it occasionally gets something wrong.

Measured over the last 400 completed looks, half came back within 24 seconds and nine in ten within 51 seconds. A few take considerably longer. Any app in this category, ours included, is doing real generation work on a server, so "instant" is not a promise worth believing from anyone.

What it does not do

Worth knowing before you download:

  • Android only. There is no iOS build.
  • Stills, not video. There is no live AR mirror.
  • It is not a feasibility check. It shows a style on your face; it cannot tell you whether your own hair is long enough or dense enough to achieve it. Ask your braider that.
  • Results vary with your photo. A clear, front-facing, well-lit shot works; a dark, angled, half-cropped one often will not.
  • It is not free forever. Your first looks are free and you can try one before making an account. After that, credit packs start at $1.49.

How to judge any app in this category, including this one

Do not take a comparison table's word for it — including ours. Five minutes settles it:

  1. Open the app's store listing and search the description for the style you actually want. If "knotless", "locs" or "cornrows" do not appear anywhere, the catalogue was not built with you in mind.
  2. Look at the screenshots. Are the models' textures anything like yours?
  3. Install it and run one style on your own face before you spend anything.
  4. Check whether the free tier gives you a watermark-free result you could actually show a stylist.
  5. Read what the app says about your photo — and whether it says where the photo goes.

On that last point, ours: your photo is stored with your account so your results are there when you come back, and it is processed by Google's Gemini model and hosted on Cloudinary. Email support@hairstudioapp.com and we will delete your photos and results.

The bottom line

If the styles you want are braids, locs, twists, cornrows or a fade, this catalogue is deeper on them than a general beauty app is likely to be, and the styles were photographed on the textures they belong to. If what you want is hair colour experiments or makeup, a general beauty app will serve you better, and we would rather say so than take the download.

Try one look on your own selfie and judge it against whatever you are using now. That is the only comparison that means anything.
